Welcome to Kampung Ceria!

Your new life begins in a quiet Malaysian village. Explore, restore and take things at your own pace.

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. On phones, use the direction buttons.
  • Interact: Stand beside a glowing object and press SPACE or the Interact button.
  • Clear weeds: Use energy to tidy your garden and earn village spirit.
  • Farm: Prepare soil, plant seeds, water crops and harvest when ready.
  • Restore: Visit the community hall, school and bridge to contribute materials and coins.
  • Sleep: End the day, restore energy and let crops grow.

This is your first playable prototype. Your progress is saved in your browser.
